Common questions

Do I need to bring anything?

Nothing — all materials are included. Wear clothes you don't mind getting paint on, or borrow one of the studio aprons.

What if I miss a class?

Let me know in advance and I'll do my best to arrange a make-up session. Classes cannot be refunded for no-shows, but they can be rescheduled once per block.

Where is the studio?

The studio is in Amsterdam. The exact address is shared with registered students by email — it's easily reached by tram and has bike parking.

Can I buy a class as a gift?

Yes — gift vouchers are available for any programme. Contact me and I'll send a printable certificate.

My child has never painted before — is that OK?

Absolutely. The children's programmes are designed for beginners. No prior experience is needed or expected.

Do you offer group or corporate bookings?

Yes — private group sessions for birthdays, team events, or hen parties are available. Get in touch to discuss.
