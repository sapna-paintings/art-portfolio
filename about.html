The maker

Made by hand,
with love

Hello — I'm a maker of things. My studio is a corner of a old building in Amsterdam, filled with paint, thread, clay, and whatever the season brings through the window.

"Art is the slowest conversation I know how to have."

The practice

Every piece begins with material — its weight, texture, how it smells on a cold morning. I work slowly, letting the process lead. Some pieces take days; some take months. I never rush a finish.

I trained in fine arts and later spent years learning traditional textile techniques across Europe. That cross-pollination — structured craft meeting intuitive mark-making — is where my work lives.

The pieces

All works are one-of-a-kind originals. I document each piece with multiple photographs and short process videos so you can really get to know something before it enters your home.

Prices reflect materials, labour, and the reality of sustaining a slow practice. Commissions are welcome — get in touch to discuss.

The process

01

Material

Sourcing, touching, living with raw materials before a mark is made.

02

Making

Slow, iterative work. No timelines. The piece tells me when it's done.

03

Documenting

Photos, video, notes — a record of what the piece went through.

04

Releasing

Letting it go to someone who will live with it. The hardest part.
